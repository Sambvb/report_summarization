Aurora Systems Ltd. - Q3 FY2025 Earnings Release (fictional company, for demo purposes)

Aurora Systems Ltd. today announced its consolidated financial results for the third quarter of fiscal year 2025. Revenue for the quarter rose 18% YoY to $2.4 bn, driven by strong demand for the company's cloud infrastructure services and continued expansion in the enterprise software segment. Organic growth, excluding the contribution from the recently completed acquisition of DataPeak Inc., was 14%, well ahead of the company's prior guidance of 9% to 11%.

Gross margin expanded by 220 bps to 64.5%, reflecting a favourable shift in product mix toward higher-margin subscription revenue, as well as procurement efficiencies in the hardware division. Operating expenses grew at a slower rate than revenue, increasing 9% to $810 mn, which resulted in meaningful operating leverage. Operating profit increased 31% to $740 mn, and operating margin improved to 30.8% from 27.7% in the prior-year quarter.

EBITDA for the quarter stood at $905 mn, an increase of 27% YoY, while net income rose 29% to $520 mn. Diluted earnings per share (EPS) came in at $1.42, compared with $1.10 in the same quarter last year, beating the analyst consensus estimate of $1.31.

The company generated free cash flow of $610 mn during the quarter, supported by disciplined capital expenditure and improved working capital management, particularly a reduction in inventory days and faster collection of receivables. Capex was $140 mn, primarily directed toward data centre capacity in Europe and Southeast Asia.

The balance sheet remains robust. Aurora ended the quarter with cash and cash equivalents of $3.1 bn and total debt of $1.2 bn, resulting in a net cash position of $1.9 bn. The debt-to-equity ratio declined to 0.21 from 0.29 at the end of the previous fiscal year. Following the refinancing of its 2026 notes at a lower coupon, the company expects interest expense to decline by approximately $25 mn on an annualised basis. In September, a leading agency upgraded the company's credit rating to A-, citing improved liquidity and lower leverage.

Order backlog reached a record $6.8 bn, up 22% QoQ, providing strong visibility into revenue for the next four to six quarters. Remaining performance obligations under multi-year cloud contracts increased 35%. The company also reported market share gains in its core enterprise storage market, according to independent industry data.

The integration of DataPeak Inc. is progressing ahead of schedule. Cost synergies of $40 mn have already been realised against a full-year target of $60 mn. Goodwill of $1.1 bn was recorded on the balance sheet in connection with the acquisition, and management does not see any indication of impairment.

Despite the strong performance, management noted some headwinds. Foreign exchange movements, particularly the strengthening of the US dollar against the euro and the yen, reduced reported revenue by approximately 2 percentage points. Wage inflation in key engineering markets also continued to put modest pressure on operating expenses. In addition, the hardware segment saw a 4% decline in unit volumes due to softer demand from small and medium-sized businesses.

Reflecting the strong year-to-date results, the Board of Directors approved a 15% increase in the quarterly dividend to $0.46 per share and authorised a new $1.5 bn share buyback programme to be executed over the next 24 months.

Outlook: Aurora is raising its full-year FY2025 guidance. The company now expects revenue growth of 15% to 17% (previously 11% to 13%) and an operating margin of approximately 30% (previously 28%). Chief Executive Officer Maria Lindqvist commented: "This was an outstanding quarter. Our strategic shift toward recurring subscription revenue is clearly paying off, and we enter the final quarter of the year with record backlog and a very strong balance sheet."
